# Backstory

## Origin

Alucard was born in the shadow of a forgotten vampire keep in **Insert place where vampires live**, His mother was a traveling performer, a dancer whose grace captivated audiences and whose knowledge of herbs and folk remedies drew whispers. Some called her a healer. Others called her something darker.

Witch.

The accusation required no proof. It required only belief.

When plague touched nearby villages, the panic that followed sought a target. Her kindness became evidence. Her knowledge became suspicion. The village burned her at the stake. When Dracula came to the village looking for his beloved.
What he saw confirmed something he already believed:

Humans destroy what they fear.  
They ruin beauty.  
They deserve extinction.

Alucard survived.

He was hidden away in the apothecary owned by his mother.

---
## Knowledge of Medicine and Healing

Alucard’s mother taught him the basics of healing long before he understood why.

Her lessons were practical:

- How to clean wounds
    
- Which herbs reduced infection
    
- How to set bones
    
- How to recognize fever and poisoning
    


In the vampire court, such knowledge was unusual. Vampires valued control and power, not preservation. All of the vampires in the court where efficient in their magic.
- - -

## Childhood in the Vampire Court

The court that raised him was not sentimental. Vampires valued art and refinement. Not as expressions of humanity, but as tools of dominance. Music, dance, and etiquette became instruments of control.

Alucard learned:

- Swordplay as choreography
    
- Courtly speech as performance
    
- Emotional detachment as survival
    

He was neither servant nor child. He was an experiment of living curiosity.

It has been sometime before a dhampir was born in **insert place**. Dhampir were seen as lesser vampires.

---

## The Father’s Ideology

Alucard’s father, Dracula, viewed humanity with cold contempt. In his philosophy:

- Humans were fragile
    
- Their morals were inconsistent
    
- Their societies inevitably collapsed
    

The execution of Alucard’s mother only reinforced this belief. To Dracula, it was proof that human civilization destroyed what it could not understand.

He believed salvation required dominion.

Extinction, in his eyes, was mercy.

Alucard did not inherit this hatred.

He inherited awareness of it.

---

## Rejection and Escape

The vampire court intended for Alucard’s to transform into a vampire.

A dhampir could become a full vampire, shedding mortality in exchange for immortality and power. It was presented as an evolution.

An improvement.

A gift.

Alucard refused out of ego.

He would not become a copy of his father.

He would define himself.

He fled the court before the final ritual.

The escape was not heroic. It was chaotic. He stole what he could, avoided pursuit, and vanished into the world beyond the castle walls.

For the first time, he belonged nowhere.

- - -
## Motivation

He seeks meaning through combat.

- Combat becomes expression
    
- War becomes a performance
    
- Healing becomes service
    

He is not driven by hatred or vengeance. Every fight is an art piece and blood is the medium. A velvet red dance of antecedent and consequent.
