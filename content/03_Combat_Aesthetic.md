## Personality in combat

- Laughs when critically hit.
    
- Applauds worthy enemies.
    
- Shouts commentary mid-fight:  
    “Oh, that was almost beautiful! Try again!”
    
- Takes unnecessary risks to make moments flashier.
    
- Always positions himself where everyone can see him.

He doesn’t want clean victories.

He wants unforgettable ones.

## Battle Cry Ideas

- “Let us paint something magnificent!”
    
- “Music! Oh — we’ll make our own!”
    
- “Bleed with rhythm!”
    
- “Don’t die yet — the crescendo hasn’t hit!”
