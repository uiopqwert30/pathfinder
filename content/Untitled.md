# The Witch and the Lord of Night

Valerio’s mother was not merely a performer in **Ustalav**.

She was _suspected_.

She healed the sick too well.  
Knew herbs that shouldn’t grow locally.  
Spoke to crows that lingered too long.

Whispers spread: witch.

When plague struck nearby villages, fear needed a target.

She was taken.

Not by vampires.

By humans.

Burned on suspicion.

Valerio was hidden away in the vampire court when it happened.

His father — a powerful vampire lord — did not intervene.

Not because he couldn’t.

Because he watched.

And what he saw confirmed something he already believed:

Humans destroy what they fear.  
They ruin beauty.  
They deserve extinction.

From that day on, he loathed humanity with the cold, philosophical hatred of someone who thinks they are making the world better by ending it.

Very Dracula.
